# FAQ

Q: Do you plan to release palladium on [insert version or loader]? 
<br />
A: Palladium will be 100% available over time on versions 1.20 and above on:
- neoforge
- quilt
- fabric
- forge(<1.21)
<br />

Q: Curseforge release?
<br />
A: Maybe, when I have a little more free time.

Q: The mod does not give the effect as in the tests, why, is it cheating?
<br />
A: The effect depends on the number of mods, pc configuration and much more, do not expect that it will be identical to the tests above.

Q: Will you support 1.20 or any other version when it becomes obsolete? 
<br />
A: Yes, there will be bug-fixes for sure, but the priority will be lower than popular versions.

Q: Updates take a long time to come out, why is that?
<br />
A: At the moment, in addition to GPUTape, H+, Palladium and ToadLib, I have two rather large mods, and I work on all of them alone.
